## 📘 What the subject covers

> **Matemáticas Avanzadas (780013)** · 6 ECTS · 2nd year · 1st semester · contents from the 2025-26 teaching guide · [official subject page](https://www.uah.es/es/estudios/estudios-oficiales/grados/asignatura/Matematicas-Avanzadas-780013/)

| Unit | Hours | Topics |
|---|---|---|
| Integer and modular arithmetic | 14 h | Divisibility, GCD and the (extended) Euclidean algorithm · Diophantine equations, fundamental theorem of arithmetic · Congruences and systems of congruences · The RSA public-key cryptosystem |
| Polynomials, finite fields and interpolation | 22 h | Polynomial rings over a field · Finite fields and their multiplicative group · Taylor, Lagrange, Newton and Hermite interpolation, divided differences |
| Functions of several variables | 20 h | Limits, continuity, partial and directional derivatives · Tangent plane and implicit differentiation · Relative, absolute and constrained extrema: Hessian, Lagrange multipliers · Multiple integrals, change of variables, areas and volumes |
